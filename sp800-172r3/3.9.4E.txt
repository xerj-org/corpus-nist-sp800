id: SP_800_172_3_0_0_03.09.04E
title: Citizenship Requirements
family: Personnel Security (SP_800_172_3_0_0_3.9)
class: security_requirement

Statement:
Verify that individuals accessing a system that processes, stores, or transmits CUI meet [param: A.03.09.04E.ODP.01]. 

Guidance:
Organizations may determine that individuals who need access to CUI associated with a high value asset or critical program require U.S. citizenship status. This requirement enhances SP 800-171 requirement 03.09.01.
  - (includes preempt)
  
  Ensure that the threat event does not have an impact.
    - Reduce likelihood of occurrence and/or reduce likelihood of impact.
    - The adversary’s efforts or resources cannot be applied or are wasted.

Parameters:
- A.03.09.04E.ODP.01
  guideline: Citizenship requirements to be met by individuals to access a system processing, storing, or transmitting CUI are defined.
