id: SP_800_172_3_0_0_03.03.02E
title: Real-Time Alerts for Audit Processing Failures
family: Audit and Accountability (SP_800_172_3_0_0_3.3)
class: security_requirement

Statement:
Provide an alert within [real-time period] to [personnel, roles, and/or locations] when the following audit failure events occur: [audit logging failure events]. 

Guidance:
Alerts provide organizations with urgent messages. Real-time alerts provide these messages at information technology speed (i.e., the time from event detection to alert occurs in seconds or less). This requirement enhances SP 800-171 requirement 03.03.04.
  - (includes preempt)
  
  Ensure that the threat event does not have an impact.
    - Reduce likelihood of occurrence and/or reduce likelihood of impact.
    - The adversary’s efforts or resources cannot be applied or are wasted.
  - (includes exert)
  
  Make it more difficult for threat events to cause adverse impacts or consequences.
    - Reduce likelihood of impact and reduce level of impact.
    - Adversary activities are restricted in scope, fail to achieve full effect, do not take place in accordance with adversary timeline, or require greater resources than adversary had planned.

Parameters:
- A.03.03.02E.ODP.01 | label: "real-time period"
  guideline: real-time period requiring alerts when audit failure events (defined in A.03.03.02E.ODP.03) occur is defined.
- A.03.03.02E.ODP.02 | label: "personnel, roles, and/or locations"
  guideline: personnel, roles, and/or locations to be alerted in real time when audit failure events (defined in A.03.03.02E.ODP.03) occur are defined.
- A.03.03.02E.ODP.03 | label: "audit logging failure events"
  guideline: audit logging failure events requiring real-time alerts are defined.
