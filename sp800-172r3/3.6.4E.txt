id: SP_800_172_3_0_0_03.06.04E
title: Automated Tracking, Data Collection, and Analysis for Incident Monitoring
family: Incident Response (SP_800_172_3_0_0_3.6)
class: security_requirement

Statement:
Track incidents and collect and analyze incident information using [organization-defined automated mechanisms]. 

Guidance:
Automated mechanisms for tracking incidents and collecting and analyzing incident information include electronic databases of incidents and network monitoring devices. This requirement enhances SP 800-171 requirement 03.06.02.
  - (includes detect and reveal)
  
  Reduce risk due to ignorance of threat events and possible replicated or similar threat events in the same or similar environments.
    - Reduce likelihood of impact.
    - The adversary loses the advantage of stealth as defenders are better prepared by developing and sharing threat intelligence.

- A.03.06.04E.ODP.01 | label: "automated mechanisms"
  guideline: automated mechanisms used to track incidents are defined.
- A.03.06.04E.ODP.02 | label: "automated mechanisms"
  guideline: automated mechanisms used to collect incident information are defined.
- A.03.06.04E.ODP.03 | label: "automated mechanisms"
  guideline: automated mechanisms used to analyze incident information are defined.
