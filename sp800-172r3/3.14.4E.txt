id: SP_800_172_3_0_0_03.14.04E
title: Refresh From Trusted Sources
family: System and Information Integrity (SP_800_172_3_0_0_3.14)
class: security_requirement

Statement:
Obtain software and data employed during system component and service refreshes from the following trusted sources: [trusted sources]. 

Guidance:
Trusted sources include software and data from write-once, read-only media or from selected offline secure storage facilities. This requirement does not enhance a specific requirement in SP 800-171 but can be used to strengthen the protection of CUI associated with critical programs or high value assets.
  - (includes preempt)
  
  Ensure that the threat event does not have an impact.
    - Reduce likelihood of occurrence and/or reduce likelihood of impact.
    - The adversary’s efforts or resources cannot be applied or are wasted.
  - (includes exert)
  
  Make it more difficult for threat events to cause adverse impacts or consequences.
    - Reduce likelihood of impact and reduce level of impact.
    - Adversary activities are restricted in scope, fail to achieve full effect, do not take place in accordance with adversary timeline, or require greater resources than adversary had planned.

Parameters:
- A.03.14.04E.ODP.01 | label: "trusted sources"
  guideline: trusted sources to obtain software and data for system component and service refreshes are defined.
