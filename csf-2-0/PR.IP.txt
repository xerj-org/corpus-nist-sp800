id: PR.IP
title: Information Protection Processes and Procedures
family: PROTECT (PR)
class: category
family-overview: Safeguards to manage the organization's cybersecurity risks are used

Statement:

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-01 - PR.IP-01

Statement:
A baseline configuration of information technology/industrial control systems is created and maintained incorporating security principles (e.g. concept of least functionality)

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-02 - PR.IP-02

Statement:
A System Development Life Cycle to manage systems is implemented

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-03 - PR.IP-03

Statement:
Configuration change control processes are in place

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-04 - PR.IP-04

Statement:
Backups of information are conducted, maintained, and tested

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-05 - PR.IP-05

Statement:
Policy and regulations regarding the physical operating environment for organizational assets are met

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-06 - PR.IP-06

Statement:
Data is destroyed according to policy

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-07 - PR.IP-07

Statement:
Protection processes are improved

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-08 - PR.IP-08

Statement:
Effectiveness of protection technologies is shared

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-09 - PR.IP-09

Statement:
Response plans (Incident Response and Business Continuity) and recovery plans (Incident Recovery and Disaster Recovery) are in place and managed

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-10 - PR.IP-10

Statement:
Response and recovery plans are tested

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-11 - PR.IP-11

Statement:
Cybersecurity is included in human resources practices (e.g., deprovisioning, personnel screening)

========================================================================
Child control PR.IP-12 - PR.IP-12

Statement:
A vulnerability management plan is developed and implemented
