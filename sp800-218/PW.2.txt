id: PW.2
title: Review the Software Design to Verify Compliance with Security Requirements and Risk Information
family: Produce Well-Secured Software (PW)
class: practice
family-overview: Organizations should produce well-secured software with minimal security vulnerabilities in its releases.

Statement:
Help ensure that the software will meet the security requirements and satisfactorily address the identified risk information.

========================================================================
Child control PW.2.1 - PW.2.1

Statement:
Have 1) a qualified person (or people) who were not involved with the design and/or 2) automated processes instantiated in the toolchain review the software design to confirm and enforce that it meets all of the security requirements and satisfactorily addresses the identified risk information.

Example:
Review the software design to confirm that it addresses applicable security requirements.

Example:
Review the risk models created during software design to determine if they appear to adequately identify the risks.

Example:
Review the software design to confirm that it satisfactorily addresses the risks identified by the risk models.

Example:
Have the software’s designer correct failures to meet the requirements.

Example:
Change the design and/or the risk response strategy if the security requirements cannot be met.

Example:
Record the findings of design reviews to serve as artifacts (e.g., in the software specification, in the issue tracking system, in the threat model).
