id: RV.3
title: Analyze Vulnerabilities to Identify Their Root Causes
family: Respond to Vulnerabilities (RV)
class: practice
family-overview: Organizations should identify residual vulnerabilities in software releases and respond appropriately to address those vulnerabilities and prevent similar ones from occurring in the future

Statement:
Help reduce the frequency of vulnerabilities in the future.

========================================================================
Child control RV.3.1 - RV.3.1

Statement:
Analyze identified vulnerabilities to determine their root causes.

Example:
Record the root cause of discovered issues.

Example:
Record lessons learned through root cause analysis in a wiki that developers can access and search.

Statement:

Example:
Record lessons learned through root cause analysis in a wiki that developers can access and search.

Example:

Example:
