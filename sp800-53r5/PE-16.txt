id: pe-16
title: Delivery and Removal
family: Physical and Environmental Protection (pe)
class: SP800-53

Statement:
  a. Authorize and control [organization-defined types of system components] entering and exiting the facility; and
  b. Maintain records of the system components.

Guidance:
Enforcing authorizations for entry and exit of system components may require restricting access to delivery areas and isolating the areas from the system and media libraries.

Parameters:
- pe-16_prm_1 | label: "organization-defined types of system components"
- pe-16_odp.01 | label: "types of system components"
  guideline: types of system components to be authorized and controlled when entering the facility are defined;
- pe-16_odp.02 | label: "types of system components"
  guideline: types of system components to be authorized and controlled when exiting the facility are defined;
