id: sc-51
title: Hardware-based Protection
family: System and Communications Protection (sc)
class: SP800-53

Statement:
  a. Employ hardware-based, write-protect for [system firmware components] ; and
  b. Implement specific procedures for [authorized individuals] to manually disable hardware write-protect for firmware modifications and re-enable the write-protect prior to returning to operational mode.

Guidance:
None.

Parameters:
- sc-51_odp.01 | label: "system firmware components"
  guideline: system firmware components requiring hardware-based write-protect are defined;
- sc-51_odp.02 | label: "authorized individuals"
  guideline: authorized individuals requiring procedures for disabling and re-enabling hardware write-protect are defined;
