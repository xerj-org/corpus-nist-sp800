id: sc-6
title: Resource Availability
family: System and Communications Protection (sc)
class: SP800-53

Statement:
Protect the availability of resources by allocating [resources] by [Selection (one-or-more): priority; quota; {{ insert: param, sc-06_odp.03 }} ].

Guidance:
Priority protection prevents lower-priority processes from delaying or interfering with the system that services higher-priority processes. Quotas prevent users or processes from obtaining more than predetermined amounts of resources.

Parameters:
- sc-06_odp.01 | label: "resources"
  guideline: resources to be allocated to protect the availability of resources are defined;
- sc-06_odp.02 | selection (one-or-more): priority; quota; {{ insert: param, sc-06_odp.03 }} 
- sc-06_odp.03 | label: "controls"
  guideline: controls to protect the availability of resources are defined (if selected);
