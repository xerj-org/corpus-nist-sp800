id: SP_800_171_03.13.11
title: Cryptographic Protection
family: System and Communications Protection (SP_800_171_03.13)
class: requirement

Statement:
Implement the following types of cryptography to protect the confidentiality of CUI: [types of cryptography].

Guidance:
Cryptography is implemented in accordance with applicable laws, Executive Orders, directives, regulations, policies, standards, and guidelines. FIPS-validated cryptography is recommended for the protection of CUI.

Parameters:
- A.03.13.11.ODP.01 | label: "types of cryptography"
  guideline: the types of cryptography for protecting the confidentiality of CUI are defined.
